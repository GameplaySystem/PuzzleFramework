## Design Decisions

- Board topology and modular activation remain reusable framework systems; concrete cell art and participation mapping remain game-owned.
- URP, stencil shaders/materials, DOTween, hole meshes, and completion animation remain prototype-owned.
- `GridWorldLayout.BoardOrigin` remains the center of cell `(0,0)`; centered construction derives that origin from logical dimensions rather than changing coordinate meaning.
- Board centering uses logical rectangular bounds, not the centroid of visible/participating cells, so obstacles and internal holes do not shift the level.
- Full-hole closing alignment is presentation-only and does not mutate committed coordinates or occupancy.
